# Privacy policy (draft — review with legal counsel before publishing)

_Last updated: 2026-10-15_

Loop Factory ("we") makes a puzzle game. This policy explains what we collect and why. We designed
the game to work without an account and to collect as little as possible.

## What we collect
| Data | When | Why | Kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| Random account id + server-issued credentials | when cloud sync is on (default) | to back up and sync your progress | until you delete your account |
| Game progress: levels solved, best rotations, move replays of completed puzzles, settings | cloud sync | sync, verifying leaderboard results | until deletion; sync logs 90 days |
| Display name you choose (or a random one) | cloud sync | shown on leaderboards | until deletion |
| Friend challenges you create or play: the puzzle, your verified rotation count and move replay | only when you tap Challenge or open a code | showing the challenge (your display name and rotations — never your moves) to anyone with the code | challenge expires after 30 days; deleted with your account |
| Google/Apple account id (and email, if the provider shares it) | only if you link an account | account recovery on a new device | until deletion |
| Purchase records (product, store transaction id) | only if you buy something | restore purchases, handle refunds | as required by law |
| Anonymous gameplay analytics (levels started/finished, hints, retries, app version) | **only if you opt in** | improve puzzle difficulty | per provider retention (≤ 1 year) |

We do **not** collect: your name, phone number, contacts, precise or coarse location, advertising
IDs or device hardware IDs. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

## Your choices and rights
* Turn analytics and cloud sync on/off in **Settings** at any time.
* **Export** your data: Settings → Export my data.
* **Delete** your account and all server data: Settings → Delete account & data. Deletion is
  immediate and irreversible; we keep only a salted hash proving the deletion happened.
* Under India's DPDP Act 2023, GDPR and similar laws you may also contact us to access, correct
  or erase data, or to raise a grievance: privacy@loopfactory.example (placeholder).

## Children
The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from children beyond the
optional, consent-based items above. Where local law requires parental consent for analytics,
analytics stays off.

## Security
Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Credentials are stored as hashes. Access to production data
is restricted.

## Processors
Hosting: Render (database and API, Singapore region). Optional: PostHog (analytics, EU),
Sentry (crash reports). Updated list on request.

## Changes
We will update this page and the in-app link when this policy changes.
