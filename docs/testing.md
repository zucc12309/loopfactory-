## Not covered by automated tests
* On-device behaviour (touch latency, haptics, audio output, Android/iOS lifecycle events) — needs a
  physical device (iOS: [IOS_DEVICE_TEST_MATRIX.md](IOS_DEVICE_TEST_MATRIX.md)); see the checklist in [BETA_READINESS_IMPLEMENTATION.md](BETA_READINESS_IMPLEMENTATION.md#f-device-qa-checklist).
* Google/Apple ID-token verification against real providers (requires client IDs).
* Store purchase verification (requires store credentials).
